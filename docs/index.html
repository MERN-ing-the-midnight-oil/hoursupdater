    When windows closed

    Each closed 15-school-day window is when clock-time changes become contracted — or are flagged for bid or bump.

      BPS 2026–2027 school calendar

      Only student school days count toward the 15-day window. Weekends, holidays, and recesses do not.

      How the contract math works

      1. A qualifying clock-time change opens a 15-school-day window. Another change on the same assignment resets that window and adds the exact minute difference.
      2. Deltas stay exact and unrounded while the window is open.
      3. When the window closes, the accumulated difference is checked against 30 minutes:
        • Under 30 minutes → the new times lock in as contracted hours.
        • Increase of 30+ minutes → bid pending.
        • Decrease of 30+ minutes → bump eligible.
      4. Contracted hours are the daily AM + Midday + PM total, rounded once to the nearest 15 minutes at lock-in. A schedule can change without contracted hours changing if both totals round to the same quarter hour.
