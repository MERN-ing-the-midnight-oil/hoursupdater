Photograph the front and back of a blue time card. Use good lighting and hold the card still. The secretary's Reg, OT, and Tot. Hrs. math all gets automatically calculated and checked based on the Clock Record times and your route info on the sticker at the top of the front side.

Save this app to your phone

Open this page on your phone, then follow the steps for that phone.

iPhone

Use Safari.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari. It is a square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll the share menu and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Teamster Tools shows up as an icon on your home screen.

Android

Use Chrome.

  1. Tap the three dots in the top corner of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen. If you see Install app, tap that.
  3. Tap Add or Install. Teamster Tools shows up as an icon on your home screen.
