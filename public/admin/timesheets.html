Timesheets

Clock in and clock out records compared with current contracted clocks. A pay period is the 1st–15th or the 16th through the end of the month. Export downloads every driver's regular and overtime hours for the selected pay period. This page does not change any route.

Hours above and below the contracted day

AM, midday, and PM totals come from the clock records. A pair is AM when its earlier time is before 8:00, midday from 8:00 until 1:00, and PM after that. Extra is a number you type for hours that are not a clock in and clock out. Extra stays on this sheet. It is not added to regular, overtime, or the two rows copied for payroll.
