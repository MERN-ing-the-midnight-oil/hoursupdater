How this app works

Plain-language definitions for the terms you see in Routing and Admin. Click any ? next to a label in the app for the same explanation in place.

The short version

  1. Routing logs the driver’s scheduled time exactly as entered — down to the minute.
  2. That opens (or resets) a 15-school-day window while exact changes accumulate as cumulative drift.
  3. When the window closes with no further change, payroll rounding runs once and either locks in new contracted hours (under 30 minutes) or moves the route to Bid Pending (30 minutes or more).
  4. Needs Review pauses the app when a correction would quietly rewrite an outcome that was already communicated.
