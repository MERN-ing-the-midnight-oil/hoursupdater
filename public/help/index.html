How this app works

Click any ? next to a label in the app for the same explanation in place.

  1. Routing logs the driver’s scheduled time — exactly as entered, down to the minute.

    Automatically: the current schedule fills in on its own, and the exact change is calculated the moment it’s submitted.

    You can: override the calculated change if it doesn’t look right, as long as you pick a reason — every entry (and every override) is attributed to whoever made it.

  2. That opens (or resets) a 15-school-day window, while exact changes accumulate as cumulative drift.

    Automatically: the countdown starts on the very first change to a route, and any further change to that same route resets it — no one has to start or track this by hand.

    You can: open a route in the Admin queue at any time to see exactly which changes are contributing to the current total and how many days are left — nothing here needs action, it’s just there to check.

  3. When the window closes with no further change, payroll rounding runs once — either locking in new contracted hours (under 30 minutes) or moving the route to Bid Pending (30 minutes or more).

    Automatically: the rounding happens exactly once, from the exact total, the moment the window closes — never before.

    You can: generate the Change Report showing the full math behind the outcome, draft an email to the driver explaining it, and — if the route lands on Bid Pending — send Payroll a heads-up with one click.

  4. Needs Review pauses the app when a correction would change an outcome that’s already been finalized and acted on.

    Automatically: the app catches this on its own and holds the route there — any new changes to that same route wait in line rather than being processed underneath an unresolved conflict.

    You can: look at both versions side by side and decide what to do — the app won’t pick one for you. Seeing this happen is normal, not a sign anything went wrong.
